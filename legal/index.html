LOVARO · QUESTIONS FOR COUPLES

Privacy, with care.

Your conversations belong to you. Here is how Lovaro handles information when you use the app. Lovaro is developed by Fazil Qazi.

Effective 13 October 2026

Your data stays yours

Lovaro works without an account. Your names, anniversary, favourites, card history, and answers are stored on your device. We do not receive the text of your answers or your partner’s name.

Services used by the app

Firebase Remote Config supplies app settings and receives device and app information needed for that service, including country, language, time zone, operating system version, and app identifiers. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user ID and purchase information to provide and restore Pro access. Apple processes purchases and subscriptions.

Optional usage analytics

Firebase Analytics is off until you choose Allow. If enabled, we send app usage events such as deck openings, card actions, widget taps, and purchase results. Some events include deck or card identifiers. We do not send question text, answers, or partner names as event data. You can turn analytics off in Settings at any time.

Sharing and widgets

Widget data and scheduled reminders are prepared on your device. Remote play codes are shared only when you choose to send them. If you use the iOS share sheet, the content you select goes to the service you choose, under that service’s privacy practices.

Your choices

You can decline or turn off usage analytics in Settings. Deleting Lovaro removes the app’s local data from that device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. For privacy requests, use the Support link on Lovaro’s App Store page.

Tracking

Lovaro does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not engage in tracking.

Data retention

Your local data stays on your device until you delete the app. If you enable analytics, event data is retained according to Firebase's default retention settings.

Children

Lovaro is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Other providers

You can read the privacy information for Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email qazifazil59@gmail.com.
